3. What data is processed?

3.1 Photos for vocabulary recognition

When you photograph a textbook page or select a photo, this image is sent as a JPEG via an encrypted HTTPS connection to a Cloudflare Worker (our server infrastructure).

Important: An uploaded photo may contain personal data – such as names, handwritten notes, faces or other information on the photographed page. Please photograph only the vocabulary page and make sure that no people, names or personal notes are visible in the image. The app shows a corresponding notice before each scan.

Processing chain:
App → Cloudflare Worker → OpenRouter → AI model provider → OpenRouter → Cloudflare Worker → App

Storage: The provider itself does not permanently store the transmitted image, nor in a database. In the Cloudflare Worker, the image is processed only in memory to handle the request. For AI analysis, the image is transmitted to OpenRouter Inc. and the AI model provider used.

Zero Data Retention (ZDR): Zero Data Retention is enforced technically for AI processing. This means only AI provider endpoints are used that do not permanently store the transmitted inputs and generated outputs after processing is completed. In addition, the use of content for model training or improvement is prohibited (data_collection: "deny"). OpenRouter may process technical metadata of the request, such as the model used, token count, processing duration and cost information. A fallback to a non-ZDR-capable provider is technically excluded.

3.2 Vocabulary and learning progress

Recognized vocabulary, entries you created manually, and your learning progress (status, correct answers, timestamps) are stored locally on your device (SwiftData).

If you have iCloud enabled, this data is synchronized across your other devices via Apple CloudKit. The synchronized learning data is stored in the user's private iCloud environment. VokabelScan does not operate its own user database for this. Access is provided through the Apple iCloud environment made available by the operating system.

3.3 Scan quota counters

To track across devices how many free scans you have already used, the app stores a counter (e.g. “3 of 5”) in the iCloud Key-Value Store and locally on the device. The counter contains no names, email addresses or other directly identifying information. However, it is stored in the user's personal iCloud Key-Value Store and is therefore attributable to the user within the Apple ID/device relationship.

3.4 Premium subscriptions

The purchase and management of the Premium subscription takes place via Apple StoreKit. All payment and transaction data remains with Apple. The app only learns whether a subscription is active or not. No payment data is processed by the app or the provider.

4. Server-side processing (Cloudflare)

Two separate processing operations are distinguished at Cloudflare:

4.1 Provision of the AI interface (Cloudflare Worker)

Our API is operated via Cloudflare Workers. Technically necessary network and request data is processed. The uploaded image is forwarded by the Worker to OpenRouter and is not permanently stored by us there.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract performance).

4.2 Abuse protection / rate limiting (Cloudflare Workers KV)

To protect against automated abuse and unlimited API costs, your IP address – a piece of personal data – is stored together with the date as a counter (key: scans:<IP>:<date>) in Cloudflare Workers KV with each recognition request. This counter is automatically deleted after 48 hours. The IP address is not linked with other data and is not used for other purposes.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest). Legitimate interest: protection of the technical infrastructure and limiting abuse-induced API costs. Storage period: max. 48 hours.

6. Processors and third parties used

The app uses the following third-party services, which act as processors (Art. 28 GDPR) or as further recipients within the processing chain:

6.1 Apple iCloud (CloudKit)

Syncing your learning data and quota counters. The data is stored in the user's private CloudKit database and is only accessible via the Apple ID. The Apple privacy policy applies.

6.2 Apple StoreKit

Subscription handling. Payment data remains with Apple; the app only receives the subscription status (active/inactive). The Apple privacy policy applies.

6.3 Cloudflare Inc. (processor)

Operation of the API worker (processing the photo, forwarding to OpenRouter) and storage of the rate-limit counters in Workers KV. A Data Processing Addendum (DPA) is part of the contract. The Cloudflare privacy policy applies.

6.4 OpenRouter Inc. (processor)

For AI-supported vocabulary recognition, the image is transmitted to OpenRouter. OpenRouter forwards the input to the respectively used AI model provider for processing. Processing takes place under a Zero-Data-Retention configuration, so that the transmitted image data is not stored at the model provider beyond what is necessary to carry out the request. The connected model providers are further recipients within the processing chain. A DPA is incorporated via the OpenRouter terms of use. The OpenRouter privacy policy applies.

The links to the third-party privacy policies supplement this information but do not replace the information obligations here.

7. Data transfers to third countries

Cloudflare Inc. and OpenRouter Inc. are US companies. When using their services, personal data may be processed outside the European Economic Area (EEA).

Where personal data is processed outside the European Economic Area, the transfer takes place on the basis of an applicable adequacy decision by the European Commission or, where such a decision is not applicable, on the basis of appropriate safeguards pursuant to Art. 46 GDPR, in particular the Standard Contractual Clauses of the European Commission:

  • Cloudflare: EU Standard Contractual Clauses within the framework of the Data Processing Addendum.
  • OpenRouter: EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2 Controller → Processor) within the framework of the DPA.

Which additional adequacy basis applies in a specific case depends on the respective contract configuration.

10. Children and minors

The app can also be used by minors. For the core functions of the app, no data-protection consent is required as a legal basis; processing is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract performance). Where consent should be required for individual future functions, the special legal requirements for minors under Art. 8 GDPR will be taken into account. Subscription purchases are handled via Apple StoreKit.

To protect minors, the app shows a notice before each scan that only the vocabulary page should be photographed and that no people, names or personal notes may be visible in the image.
