Frequently asked questions

How does VokabelScan work?

Photograph a page from your textbook or worksheets. The app automatically recognizes the vocabulary and saves it for learning. You can practice in 5 quiz modes and your learning progress is tracked.

How many scans are free?

Free of charge are 5 scan actions (lifetime) and access to the 5 most recent scans. With Premium you expand to 50 scans per month and unlimited access to all your scans.

How do I cancel my Premium subscription?

You can cancel the subscription at any time in your device's App Store settings: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → VokabelScan → Cancel. The cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.

Is my data synchronized?

If you have iCloud enabled, your vocabulary and learning progress are synchronized across all your devices. The data lives in your private iCloud. More in the privacy policy.

Scans are not recognized

Make sure the photo is well lit and sharp and shows the vocabulary table in full. You can always enter vocabulary manually — even without a scan.
